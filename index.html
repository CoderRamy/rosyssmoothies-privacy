Rose's Smoothies - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Short version for parents: Rose's Smoothies is a game for young children. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no chat, no analytics and no tracking. It works fully offline. We do not collect, send or share any information about your child or your device.

This policy explains how the mobile game Rose's Smoothies (the "Game"), published by Crazy Beans ("we", "us"), handles information.

1. Information we collect

None. The Game does not collect, receive, store or transmit any personal information or device identifiers. It does not ask for a name, email address, photos, location, contacts or any other information. The Game does not request the internet permission and does not communicate with our servers or any third party.

2. Information stored on the device

The Game saves its progress on the device only: which drinks have been made, which fruits, friends, straws, cups and decorations have been unlocked, whether the tutorial was finished, and the sound and music settings. This information never leaves the device and is deleted when the Game's data is cleared or the Game is uninstalled. Parents can also reset it from the grown-ups' settings inside the Game.

3. Advertising, purchases and third-party services

The Game contains no advertising and no in-app purchases, and includes no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software (SDKs).

4. Links

The only link that leaves the Game is the link to this privacy policy. It is placed in the grown-ups' settings, which open only after pressing and holding a button for a few seconds (a simple parental gate).

5. Children

The Game is designed for children aged about 2 to 5 and complies with the Google Play Families Policy. Because we collect no personal information at all, no personal information from children is collected, used or disclosed.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell or share any information, because we do not collect any.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Any future version will continue to describe exactly what the Game does with information.
